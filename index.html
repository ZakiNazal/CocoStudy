CocoStudy: Marked up as you learn

An AI-powered, offline-first study companion that turns your lectures, slide decks, PDFs, and notes into interactive study guides, spaced repetition flashcards, customizable quizzes, and a personal AI tutor.

Highlights

  • Multi-format intake: PDFs, Word documents, PowerPoint decks, audio, video, or pasted text.
  • Smart study guides with outlines, LaTeX math, and code blocks.
  • Spaced repetition flashcards using the SM-2 scheduler.
  • Customizable quizzes: multiple choice, true/false, and essay.
  • A grounded AI tutor that answers from your course material.
  • Private and offline-first: your data stays in your browser.
